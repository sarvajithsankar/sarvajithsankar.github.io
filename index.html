07 / BEYOND THE SCREEN

BEYOND THE SCREEN. I like talking to people who build.

I enjoy meeting engineers, founders, and developers and asking what they're working on. Not the polished version — the parts that were annoying, the tools that surprised them, how their team actually ships. I learn a lot from those conversations, and I usually leave with something to try.

"Most of what I know about industry came from someone taking ten minutes to explain what their team actually does."
WHAT I ASK ABOUT

What they're building, what broke recently, and how the team decided to do it that way.

WHAT I ENJOY

Hand-drawn diagrams, honest trade-offs, and demos that are still a little rough around the edges.

WHAT I DO AFTER

Write a small version of the idea, break it, and see whether it holds up outside the conversation.
